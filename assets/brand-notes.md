# PhotoBridge icon

The selected “Photo arch” design combines a cobalt photo frame with a bridge-shaped landscape and a coral sun on warm ivory. It uses bold contours and generous space so the photo and bridge remain readable at small app-icon sizes.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized for native application packaging. The source artwork remains square and opaque; platforms supply their icon masks.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: one production-ready iOS app icon exploration for PhotoBridge, wireless photo backup across devices. Design A, "Photo arch": flat, extremely simple, friendly and unmistakable at 32 pixels. Opaque full-bleed warm ivory square background. Center one bold cobalt-blue rounded photo frame, generous interior negative space. Inside the frame, one solid blue flowing arch/rising landscape silhouette also reads subtly as a bridge, plus one small warm coral sun disk in the upper right. Only these three elements: frame, arch, sun. No separate transfer arrows or radio waves. Optically balanced central symbol occupies 62 percent of canvas, generous clear space all sides. Precise vector-like smooth curves, rounded joins, uniform thick frame. Calm premium consumer app identity, not a detailed illustration. No text, letters, gradients, 3D, shadows, glows, textures, mockups, extra tiles, badges or miniature objects. Render ONE square finished icon only, not a board. Output 1024 square.

The website's landscape contact sheet reuses the generated PixelBridge sample imagery; it contains no user-library photos. Device visuals are illustrative, not claims about an actual transfer session.
