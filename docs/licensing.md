copyright notice. The license text governs; this page explains the project policy
and does not add restrictions to permissions granted by that license.

## Noncommercial use

You can inspect the source, build the apps for personal noncommercial backups,
study the implementation, and make or share noncommercial modifications under
the license. Preserve the license and required notices when distributing copies.
The license also specifies permitted uses by certain noncommercial organizations
and preserves applicable fair-use rights; see its full terms.

## Commercial use

Commercial rights not granted by the license remain with the copyright holders.
Being an individual, independent developer or small business does not itself
create an exemption. Activities outside the license's permitted purposes require
a separate written commercial license. Examples include:

- Selling the app or a modified/rebranded build, including through the App Store.
- Monetizing a derivative app through subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.
- Operating a paid service based on the software.

Renaming the app or removing attribution does not remove licensing obligations.
Public access to the repository does not grant an unrestricted commercial license.
For a commercial licensing request, open an
[issue](https://github.com/qhhonx/photobridge/issues) describing the intended use;
do not include private credentials or confidential business information.
A discussion or an unanswered request is not permission. Any separate grant must
come from the copyright holders with authority over the relevant code.

## Contributions and dependencies

Third-party dependencies remain under their own licenses and notices. This policy
does not relicense their code or claim ownership of it.

Contributors retain their copyright. A pull request under the current license does
not, by itself, assign copyright or authorize the project to commercially relicense
that contributor's work. Before accepting third-party contributions, the project
will arrange a separate written contributor agreement if commercial relicensing
rights are required. Commercial grants must cover all relevant rights holders.
